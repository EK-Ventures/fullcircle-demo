AN EXPERIENCE LIKE NO OTHER

Your evening at Full Circle Theatre begins before the performance

Arrive early, meet friends and dine in spaces designed around each production. Then take your place within an audience of just 200 who surround the stage.

The actors are not distant stars on a far-off stage; they share the room with you, and at that proximity the smallest emotional inflexion is felt.

As the performance unfolds, the world around you transforms. Full Circle Theatre’s world-first immersive wrap-around auditorium, with high-definition LED walls, floor and ceiling merges peerless live theatrical performance with powerful cinematic expression, enhanced by state-of-the-art technology that serves the performance.

WHERE ART FORMS CONVERGE

Full Circle Theatre brings theatre, cinema, music, movement, visual & audio art, and immersive design together in a single live experience

Performance and storytelling lead, keeping the actors and the drama at the centre, metres from your seat, whilst technology transforms the world around you.

Unique and unforgettable storytelling, creating a life-punctuating experience.

CREATED LIVE IN LONDON. EXPERIENCED AROUND THE WORLD

Every Full Circle production is made for the intimacy of live performance and designed from the outset for cinematic capture

Performances are live-streamed to cinemas, to screens at home and to mobile devices through the Full Circle app, and remain available on demand.

From central London to a global audience, live performance and cinematic distribution form one integrated experience.

A NEW CULTURAL LANDMARK FOR LONDON

Full Circle Theatre - a home for exceptional performance

Creative collaboration and new theatrical ideas are developed within a new London destination of international standing.

An extraordinary platform for the best international creatives to envelop the audience with unique perspectives that inspire, excite and transport to other worlds - an entertainment experience with no equivalency.
