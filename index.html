A New Stage for a New Cultural Age

Full Circle Theatre is being created as a new international centre for live dramatic art.

It will bring together exceptional actors, writers, directors, filmmakers, visual artists, musicians and designers to create performances that move beyond the boundaries of the conventional stage.

In an intimate auditorium, the audience will surround the performers as entire cinematic worlds unfold around them.

The result will be theatre experienced not from a distance, but from within.

Where Art Forms Converge

Full Circle Theatre will exist at the meeting point of theatre, cinema, visual art, sound, design and contemporary technology.

Each production will begin with the artist and the story.

Technology will remain largely invisible, allowing a room to become a city, an ocean, a memory or another century without breaking the connection between actor and audience.

This is not theatre imitating cinema.
It is a new dramatic language, created live.

World-Class

Intimate

Approximately 200 people surrounding the performance, with every audience member extraordinarily close to the actors.

Transformative

Cinematic environments, spatial sound and live performance combining to transport audiences between places, periods and emotional worlds.

Unrepeatable

Every performance live, immediate and subtly different, creating an experience that exists fully only for the people present.

Created in London. Connected to the World.

London has always been a meeting place for artists, ideas and audiences from around the world.

Full Circle Theatre will build upon that tradition, presenting work with international artists and inviting global audiences to encounter a new form of live performance.

Its ambition is to become both a defining London cultural experience and an international centre for the development of dramatic art.

  • A place to discover important new work.
  • A place artists aspire to create.
  • A place audiences travel to experience.

More Than a Performance

The most memorable cultural experiences continue long after we leave them.

They change the way we see a story, an artist, a city or ourselves.

Full Circle Theatre will create performances of exceptional visual, emotional and intellectual ambition, designed to become part of the cultural memory of their audiences.

Each visit should feel significant. Each production should offer something that cannot be encountered anywhere else.

Come for the performance. Leave with a different view of the world.
