The Best Seat in the House, Every Time

Your evening at Full Circle begins before the performance

Arrive, meet, dine and enjoy the anticipation of a destination created around the production. Then enter an extraordinarily intimate theatre-in-the-round, where an audience of only 200 surrounds the performance.

The actors are not distant figures on a stage. They are only metres away. Every expression, every breath and every moment of emotion can be experienced at remarkably close quarters.

As the performance unfolds, the world around you transforms through cinematic environments, spatial sound and technology you never see.

The technology is not the performance; it enables the performance, leaving the actors, the drama and the shared human experience at the centre of everything.

Where Art Forms Converge

Full Circle brings together theatre, cinema, music, movement, visual art and immersive design within a single live experience.

The technology is never the performance. It remains invisible, allowing human performance, story and emotion to lead-while the theatrical world around the audience changes completely.

Created Live in London - Experienced Around the World

Full Circle productions are conceived for the intimacy and immediacy of live performance and designed from the outset for cinematic capture.

Performances will be shared with audiences around the world, live-streamed to cinemas, home-cinemas, TV screens, streaming platforms and mobile devices, through our proprietary app, and always available on-demand.

From an intimate 200-seat London theatre to a global audience, Full Circle brings live performance and cinematic distribution together as one integrated experience.

A New Cultural Landmark for London

Full Circle Theatre will be a home for exceptional performance, creative collaboration and new theatrical ideas - a London destination with an international audience.

Created in London and shared with the world, Full Circle will bring together artists, audiences and art forms within an intimate theatrical experience unlike any other.
